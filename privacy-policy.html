We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully to understand our practices regarding your information.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

Our App works offline and does not require registration, account creation, or the submission of personal information (such as your name, email address, or phone number). We do not directly collect, store, or process personal data from our users.

B. Information Collected Automatically via Third-Party Services

While we do not collect personal data directly, we utilize AppsFlyer, a third-party Mobile Measurement Partner (MMP), to analyze marketing performance and measure app installation attribution.

AppsFlyer may automatically collect non-personally identifiable technical information from your device, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as IDFA / Identifier for Advertisers, IDFV / Identifier for Vendors, or advertising IDs).
  • Technical details about your device (IP address, operating system version, device model, language settings).
  • App installation and performance metrics (e.g., source of download, referral campaign, app launch events).

This data is collected solely to help us understand where our app downloads come from and to optimize our advertising campaigns.

2. How We Use Information

The technical attribution data collected via AppsFlyer is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Measuring the effectiveness of our marketing and advertising campaigns.
  • Analyzing overall app installation trends and user acquisition channels.
  • Maintaining, troubleshooting, and improving the stability and functionality of the App.

3. Third-Party Privacy Policies

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. The third-party service provider we use operates under its own privacy policy:

  • AppsFlyer: You can review AppsFlyer’s privacy policy and data practices at https://www.appsflyer.com/legal/services-privacy-policy/.

4. Tracking and Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On iOS devices, we adhere to Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. AppsFlyer will only collect device identifiers (such as IDFA) for attribution purposes if you explicitly grant permission when prompted by the iOS system permission dialog.

You can manage or revoke your permission at any time in your iOS device settings under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App is designed for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so that we can take steps to remove such information.
