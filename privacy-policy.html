Crossword Puzzle: Word Game
Effective Date: August 9, 2026  |  Last Updated: August 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how IE EVGENII ALEKSANOV ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with our mobile application, Crossword Puzzle: Word Game (the "App").

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully to understand our practices regarding your information.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide Directly

Our App works offline and does not require registration, account creation, or the submission of personal information (such as your name, email address, or phone number). We do not directly collect, store, or process personal data from our users.

B. Information Collected Automatically via Third-Party Services

While we do not collect personal data directly, our App integrates third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs) for advertising, analytics, and performance monitoring:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising): Used to serve in-app advertisements. Google AdMob may automatically collect device identifiers (such as IDFA or Advertising ID), IP address, diagnostic data, and user interaction data to serve personalized or contextual non-personalized ads, depending on your consent and location settings.
  • Google Firebase (Analytics & Diagnostics): Used for app analytics, crash reporting (Crashlytics), and performance monitoring. Firebase collects anonymous usage stats, crash logs, device hardware models, and app event logs to help us ensure app stability.
  • AppsFlyer (Analytics & Attribution): Used as a Mobile Measurement Partner (MMP) to analyze marketing performance and measure app installation attribution. AppsFlyer may collect non-personally identifiable technical information (device IDs, IP address, OS version, app launch events).

2. How We Use Information

The technical data collected via third-party services is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • Serving relevant in-app advertisements (personalized or contextual).
  • Monitoring app stability, detecting crashes, and troubleshooting performance issues.
  • Measuring the effectiveness of our marketing and advertising campaigns.
  • Analyzing overall app installation trends, user retention, and feature usage.

3. Third-Party Privacy Policies

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Third-party service providers operate under their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & AdMob: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads
  • Google Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • AppsFlyer: https://www.appsflyer.com/legal/services-privacy-policy/

4. Tracking and Apple App Tracking Transparency (ATT)

On iOS devices, we adhere strictly to Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. Third-party SDKs (such as Google AdMob and AppsFlyer) will only access device identifiers (such as IDFA) for tracking or personalized advertising if you explicitly grant permission when prompted by the iOS system permission dialog.

You can manage or revoke your tracking permission at any time in your iOS device settings under Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

5. European Union (EEA/UK) & Regional Privacy Rights (GDPR)

If you reside in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have specific rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • You have the right to give, refuse, or withdraw your consent for personalized advertising and data processing at any time.
  • When required, consent choices are collected using Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) in accordance with the IAB TCF v2.2 framework.
  • You can update or revoke your choices at any time via the Privacy Settings menu option inside the App.

6. US State Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you reside in certain US states (such as California), you have the right to opt-out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising. You can exercise this choice through the privacy choices banner presented upon launching the App or via the in-app privacy settings.

7. Children's Privacy

Our App is designed for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so that we can take steps to remove such information.
